What you'll train

Complete players are built on fundamentals. Every lesson is focused, purposeful, and earned rep by rep.

Hitting

Swing mechanics, approach, and pitch recognition — taught by a switch-hitter who spent 15 years facing professional pitching.

Fielding

Footwork, glove work, and game-speed reads from the Future Stars Series' West Coast Infield Coordinator.

Speed Development

First-step quickness, acceleration, and running form that translates directly to stolen bases and range.

Baserunning

Reads, leads, and instincts — the details that turn raw speed into runs and separate smart players from the rest.
