Lesson Policy

Simple rules that protect your training time — and make sure every session counts.

Come Ready to Work

Please arrive mentally and physically ready to work. Every session is focused, purposeful training time — show up prepared to get better.

Monthly Packages

Lesson packages are monthly and should be completed within that month.

  • 4 × 30-minute package — $150, completed within the month
  • 4 × 1-hour package — $250, completed within the month

Unused Lessons

Unused lessons do not roll over to the following month unless a serious emergency or unforeseen circumstance is approved by the coaches.

Questions?

If anything about scheduling or packages is unclear, reach out before booking — we're happy to help.
