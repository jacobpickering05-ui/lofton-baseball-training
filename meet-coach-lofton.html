The Career

Every lesson at LBT is backed by three decades inside professional baseball — as a player, and as a coach trusted by an MLB organization.

1993

Drafted by the Cincinnati Reds

Selected in the 1993 MLB Draft out of Los Angeles, launching a professional career at 19 years old.

15 Years

A Professional's Grind

Fifteen years in professional baseball — a switch-hitting shortstop battling through the Reds, Red Sox, and Orioles systems and multiple pro leagues. Nothing was handed to him. Everything was earned.

2001

The Big Leagues — Boston Red Sox

Called up to the Majors as a shortstop for the Boston Red Sox. Coach Lofton knows exactly what it takes to reach the highest level of the game — because he stood on that field.

2014–17

Coach, Seattle Mariners Organization

Hired by the Seattle Mariners to develop their professional prospects — four seasons coaching across the Mariners' minor league system, including as a hitting coach, shaping the next wave of big leaguers.

Today

West Coast Infield Coordinator, New Balance Future Stars Series

A national platform for elite draft-caliber prospects — Coach Lofton leads infield development for the West Coast and managed the 2022 Future Stars Series Main Event.

Now

Coach, Paradise Honors High School

Coaching the Panthers in Surprise, Arizona — 2026 4A Skyline Region Champions.

Coaching Philosophy

I believe in developing complete baseball players through discipline, hard work, and attention to detail.

My focus is on teaching the fundamentals of hitting, fielding, speed development, and baserunning while helping players build confidence through preparation. My goal is to create athletes who play the game the right way — with effort, accountability, and a team-first mindset. I challenge players to train with purpose so they can compete with confidence.

Train harder than you play. When game day arrives, there should be no doubt in your mind — because you've already put in the work. Confidence isn't something you hope for; it's something you earn through preparation, discipline, and repetition.

Championships aren't won on game day; they're built during the hours no one is watching. Outwork your competition every day, and you'll never have to question your ability when the lights come on.

Train with purpose. Play with confidence.
— Coach James Lofton Jr.
